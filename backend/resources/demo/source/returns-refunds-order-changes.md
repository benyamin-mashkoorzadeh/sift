# Returns, Refunds & Order Changes

Lumenfield Supply - Customer Policy

## Returns

Unused products may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Products must be in resalable condition and include their original accessories and proof of purchase. Opened packaging is acceptable when the product itself is unused and complete.

Final-sale items, gift cards, personalized products, and products damaged through misuse cannot be returned. Customers should contact Lumenfield Support before sending a return so the team can provide return instructions and a reference number.

## Return shipping

Lumenfield Supply covers return shipping when an item is damaged, defective, or incorrect. For preference-based returns, the customer is responsible for return shipping unless local law requires otherwise.

# Refunds and Order Changes

## Refund timing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned product is inspected. A bank or card issuer may require additional time to display the credit. Original delivery fees are not refunded unless the order was damaged, defective, incorrect, or never delivered.

## Order cancellation

An order may be cancelled within 30 minutes of placement, provided fulfillment has not started. After that window, Lumenfield Support will try to stop the shipment but cannot guarantee cancellation. If the order has already shipped, the customer may use the standard return process after delivery.

## Changing an order

Product selections and quantities cannot be edited after fulfillment starts. A delivery address may be corrected only before the carrier receives the parcel. Customers should contact Support immediately with the order number and corrected details.
