# Warranty & Support

Lumenfield Supply - Customer Policy

## Limited warranty

Lumenfield Supply products include a 12-month limited warranty beginning on the original delivery date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship under normal intended use.

The warranty does not cover ordinary wear, accidental damage, misuse, improper storage, unauthorized repair, cosmetic changes that do not affect function, or damage caused by using incompatible accessories.

## Making a warranty claim

Contact Lumenfield Support with the order number, a description of the problem, and clear photos or video when useful. Support may request troubleshooting steps before approving a repair, replacement, or refund. Proof of purchase is required.

# Support Availability

## Contact hours

Lumenfield Support is available Monday through Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 UTC, excluding public holidays. Requests can be submitted at any time and are reviewed during support hours.

The team aims to send an initial response within one business day. Complex warranty investigations and carrier claims may require additional time, but Support will provide progress updates.

## Information to include

For faster help, customers should include their order number, the email address used for the order, a concise description of the issue, and any relevant photos. Payment card numbers and account passwords must never be sent to Support.
