# Shipping & Delivery

Lumenfield Supply - Customer Policy

## Delivery times

Orders normally leave the warehouse within 1 to 2 business days. Standard domestic delivery usually takes 3 to 5 business days after dispatch. Express domestic delivery usually takes 1 to 2 business days after dispatch. Weekends and public holidays are not business days.

Delivery estimates can be affected by severe weather, carrier disruption, or remote destinations. The tracking page remains the most current source for the parcel's status.

## Tracking

A shipping confirmation email containing the carrier and tracking link is sent when the order leaves the warehouse. Tracking may take up to 24 hours to show its first scan.

# Delivery Problems

## Address changes

A delivery address may be corrected before the parcel is transferred to the carrier. Lumenfield Supply cannot guarantee rerouting after dispatch. Customers should contact Support immediately and include the order number and complete corrected address.

## Missing deliveries

If tracking shows delivered but the parcel cannot be found, the customer should check safe locations and ask household members or neighbors. If it remains missing after 24 hours, contact Support. For tracking that has not moved for 5 business days, Support can open a carrier investigation.

## Damaged or incorrect products

A damaged or incorrect product must be reported within 7 days of delivery. The customer should provide the order number, a short description, and clear photos of the product and shipping packaging. Lumenfield Supply will arrange a replacement or refund after reviewing the report and will provide return shipping when the original item must be sent back.
